Containment structures are the final barrier between a nuclear reactor's radioactive materials and the environment. Evolving from the simple steel spheres of the 1950s to the double-walled reinforced concrete structures of modern plants, containment design reflects the nuclear industry's deepening understanding of severe accident scenarios. The 9 containment types below represent distinct engineering philosophies for managing reactor overpressure events.
